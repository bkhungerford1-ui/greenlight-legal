Green Light Solutions

Green Light Solutions plans, schedules and publishes social media posts for the local businesses it manages, such as event companies and car detailers.

Each business connects its own Instagram, Facebook, Threads, TikTok, YouTube, LinkedIn and Google Business Profile accounts. Green Light publishes only the posts the business approves. TikTok videos are sent to the business's TikTok inbox as drafts, and the business posts them in the TikTok app. Reels are uploaded to the business's YouTube channel as Shorts.
