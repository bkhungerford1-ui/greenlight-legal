Green Light Solutions Privacy Policy

Last updated October 22, 2026

Who we are

Green Light Solutions (operated by Brian Hungerford) plans, schedules and publishes social media posts for local businesses that hire us. This policy covers the Green Light app, including its connections to TikTok, YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, Threads, LinkedIn and Google Business Profile.

What we collect

  • Sign-in tokens that a business gives us when it connects its own accounts (for example TikTok or YouTube). We never see or store passwords.
  • Basic account details returned by those services: account or channel ID, display name, and follower or subscriber counts.
  • The posts, captions, pictures and videos the business provides or approves, and the results of publishing them (post IDs, links, view and engagement counts).
  • Contact details of the business owner we work with, such as name and email address.

How we use it

  • To publish only the posts and videos the business has approved, to the accounts the business connected.
  • For TikTok: to send the business's approved videos to its own TikTok inbox as drafts. The business finishes and posts them in the TikTok app.
  • For YouTube: to upload the business's approved videos to its own channel as Shorts.
  • To show the business how its posts perform.

We do not sell personal information, use it for advertising profiles, or share it with anyone except the platforms the business asked us to post to.

Where it is kept

Data is stored in the business's own Google workspace (Google Sheets and Google Apps Script) managed by Green Light Solutions. Sign-in tokens are kept in Google Apps Script properties and are not shown on screen after they are saved.

How long we keep it

We keep data while we manage the business's accounts. When a business disconnects an account, its tokens are deleted. When our work ends, we delete the workspace data on request, within 30 days.

Your choices

A business can disconnect TikTok, YouTube or any other account at any time from Green Light, or remove Green Light's access in that service's own settings (for example TikTok: Settings, Security, Manage app permissions; Google: myaccount.google.com/permissions). To ask what we hold or to have it deleted, email bkhungerford1@gmail.com.

Google user data

Our use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube data is used only to upload the business's own videos and read its channel name and subscriber count. See the YouTube Terms of Service (https://www.youtube.com/t/terms) and the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).

Children

Green Light is a business tool and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes and contact

We will update this page if our practices change. Questions: bkhungerford1@gmail.com.
